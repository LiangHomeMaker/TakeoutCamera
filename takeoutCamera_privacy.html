Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026 · Effective: November 10, 2026

Privacy Policy Terms of Use

The developer of this app ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how Takeout Watermark (the "App") handles information when you take delivery photos, add watermarks, record voice notes, manage history, and share photos, and how you can manage that information.

Privacy highlights

No account is required. Photos, voice notes, history, and templates are stored on your device and are not automatically uploaded by the App to a developer-operated server. Automatic address lookup uses Apple system services. Sharing, device backups, and photo syncing may transfer content to services you choose.

1. Information We Process

  • Photos and watermark content: Photos you take, together with the capture date, time, address, delivery position, and selected text notes, are used to create watermarked photos and save and display delivery records. Faces, door numbers, and order labels in photos may also contain personal information. Capture only what you need.
  • Location: With your permission, the App obtains your device location while you use the camera screen and uses Apple's Core Location geocoding service to turn coordinates into a readable address. Precise location may be sensitive personal information and is used only to display the current address and add it to photo watermarks. Disclosure of a location or address could reveal delivery destinations or your activities. You may deny location access, disable Precise Location, or enter an address manually. The App does not provide continuous background location tracking or route history.
  • Voice notes: When you choose to record and grant microphone access, the App records audio and stores it on your device for association with delivery photos and local playback. Recordings are not used for speech recognition or voiceprint identification and are not uploaded to a developer-operated server.
  • Templates and settings: Delivery position templates, quick note templates, watermark styles, sharing preview preferences, and other settings are saved on your device for future use. A manually entered address is used for the current capture and is saved with the corresponding record when you save the photo.
  • Support correspondence: If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide. We use it only to handle your inquiry, troubleshoot an issue, or respond to a privacy request. Please avoid sending unnecessary order details, recipient information, or other sensitive information.

The current version has no account registration, advertising, behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking services. It does not access your contacts, health data, or personal information unrelated to its photo features.

2. Device Permissions and Your Choices

  • Camera: Used to take delivery photos. If you deny access, you cannot take photos in the App, but you can still manage existing history and settings.
  • Location While Using the App: Used to obtain an address for watermarks automatically. You can still take photos and enter an address manually without this permission. Disabling Precise Location may reduce address accuracy.
  • Microphone: Used when you choose to record a voice note. Denying access does not affect photo capture or text watermarks.
  • Add Photos Only: Used to save watermarked photos to your system photo library. The current save flow requests add-only access and does not read other photos in your library. If you deny access, a photo can still be saved to in-app history, but the save-to-Photos step cannot be completed.

You can manage or revoke these permissions in the App's settings in iOS Settings. Revoking a permission stops future access for the relevant feature; it does not automatically delete existing photos, recordings, or history.

3. Storage and Retention

History photos and their dates, addresses, delivery positions, and notes are stored locally in the App. Recordings are stored as local audio files, and templates and settings are saved in the App's preferences on your device. History, associated recordings, and templates remain until you delete them or delete the App and its data through iOS. Settings remain until you change them or delete the App's data.

We do not provide a developer-operated cloud copy of this content. If you enable iCloud Photos, device backups, or another syncing service, content may be backed up or synced according to your system settings. Storage locations, retention, and deletion are governed by the relevant service. Removing the App does not delete copies in Photos, cloud backups, or services to which you have shared content.

Information you voluntarily send by email is retained only as long as needed to handle the matter. We delete it once the matter is resolved and further retention is unnecessary, unless applicable law requires otherwise.

4. Third-Party Services and Sharing

Apple system services: Automatic address lookup processes location coordinates through the system geocoding service and may require a network connection. See Apple's Privacy Policy and Apple's geocoding documentation. You can turn off location access and enter an address manually if you do not want to use automatic lookup.

Sharing you initiate: The App uses the iOS share sheet to pass a selected watermarked photo to the app or recipient you choose. Enabling the sharing preview only displays a preview; it does not automatically send a photo. The current photo-sharing feature does not attach voice notes. Recipients can see the address, time, and other content embedded in the image. Their subsequent handling of the image is subject to their own rules.

Components: The project includes the Lottie animation component for interface animations. The current integration does not provide photos, locations, or recordings to that component's developer and does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

We do not sell your personal information or provide local photos or recordings to third parties for marketing. Apart from sharing you choose, system service processing, and disclosures required or permitted by law, we do not disclose your information to others. If we introduce new processing activities, we will provide notice and obtain any consent required by applicable law.

5. Access, Correction, and Deletion

  • Use History to view saved photos and records, delete an individual record, or clear all history. When a record is deleted, the App also attempts to delete its associated voice note file.
  • Correct the address, delivery position, or note before taking a photo. Edit or delete delivery position and quick note templates in Settings. Changing a template does not change watermarks already embedded in photos.
  • Export the current photo using the system sharing option in its preview. Photos already saved to your system photo library can be managed and exported through the Photos app.
  • Delete photo-library copies separately in Photos. If needed, also remove copies from Recently Deleted, cloud backups, and services to which you shared them.
  • To remove all local App data, delete the App and its data through iOS. The Offload App option, which preserves app data, does not serve the same purpose.

There is no account to close. To request access to, a copy of, correction of, or deletion of information sent through support email, withdraw relevant consent, or ask a privacy question, contact us below. We will verify your identity only as necessary to handle the request and respond in accordance with applicable law. The developer cannot remotely access or delete content stored only on your device.

6. Information Security

The App uses the iOS app sandbox and system permissions to restrict access and enables system file protection for the history file. Please secure your device with a passcode and manage your backups carefully. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. If an incident affects personal information, we will take remedial measures and provide notices as required by applicable law.

Before using or sharing photos, addresses, order labels, or recordings that contain information about others, check that you have a lawful basis and that the information is necessary.

7. Children and Minors

The App is primarily intended for delivery work and is not specifically directed at children. Minors should read this policy and use the App with a parent or guardian's guidance. Where applicable law requires parental consent, including for processing the personal information of children under 14 in jurisdictions with that requirement, such consent must be obtained. If you believe we have improperly received a child's personal information through support correspondence or otherwise, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as features change and will revise the date shown above. We will provide appropriate notice of material changes to processing purposes, information categories, or your rights. Where renewed consent is required by law, we will obtain it before the relevant processing begins.
